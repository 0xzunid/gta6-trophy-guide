    Road to the Platinum: Vice Got a Price!

    About 200 hours, all offline, all in one playthrough. Nothing is permanently missable except possibly one meditation — do that in Chapter 1 and the rest can wait until free roam. Work the stages in order; every trophy below links to its guide.

      Counts come from the in-game 100% checklist as published by PowerPyx and GTABase: 35 requirements, most asking for part of a set, not the whole thing. The checklist itself only appears after the story, under Progress, then Stats, then 100% Completion — but everything you do before then already counts.

      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy is possibly missable. Every other trophy can be earned in free roam after the story, and the 100% checklist is fully completable after the credits.

      Why the rest are safe

      • All 12 story trophies unlock by playing the main missions — none can be skipped.
      • Every side quest, robbery, collectible and activity stays open after the final mission.
      • The 100% checklist (A Long Road) has no missable requirement, per GTABase and PowerPyx.
      • One warning that is not a missable trophy: the Irredeemable criminal profile is permanent — see Actions Speak Louder before you go on a rampage.
